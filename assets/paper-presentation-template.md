# Academic Paper Presentation Template

## Purpose

This template defines the standard structure for graduate research group meeting presentations.

The goal is not to summarize the paper section by section, but to reconstruct the research story.

---

# Slide 1: Title

## Content

Include:

- Paper title.
- Authors.
- Conference/journal.
- Year.
- Presenter information.

## Design

Use:

- Minimal title page.
- Optional paper key figure.

Avoid:

- Large decorative backgrounds.

---

# Slide 2: Research Background

## Goal

Explain:

Why is this problem important?

## Content

Include:

- Application scenario.
- Current challenges.
- Existing limitations.

## Visual

Prefer:

- Concept illustration.
- Research scenario diagram.

---

# Slide 3: Motivation and Research Gap

## Goal

Explain:

Why are existing methods insufficient?

## Content

Structure:

Existing methods:

↓

Limitations:

↓

Need for new solution:

## Visual

Prefer:

- Comparison diagram.
- Limitation table.

---

# Slide 4: Related Work

## Goal

Position the paper in the research landscape.

## Content

Include:

- Main categories.
- Representative methods.
- Remaining problems.

## Visual

Prefer:

- Timeline.
- Taxonomy diagram.
- Comparison table.

---

# Slide 5: Problem Definition

## Goal

Clearly define the research problem.

## Content

Include:

- Task definition.
- Threat model if applicable.
- Research assumptions.

## Visual

Prefer:

- Problem illustration.
- Input-output diagram.

---

# Slide 6: Main Contribution

## Goal

Explain what the paper contributes.

## Content

Usually include:

1. New benchmark/dataset.
2. New method/framework.
3. New evaluation or analysis.

## Visual

Prefer:

- Contribution overview diagram.

---

# Slide 7: Proposed Method Overview

## Goal

Explain the complete framework.

## Content

Include:

- Overall architecture.
- Main modules.
- Data/process flow.

## Visual

Priority:

1. Original paper framework figure.
2. Simplified annotated version.

---

# Slide 8-10: Technical Details

## Goal

Explain key innovations.

Each slide should contain:

- One technical idea.
- One supporting figure.

Avoid:

- Showing all formulas.
- Copying algorithm descriptions.

---

# Slide 11: Experimental Setup

## Content

Include:

- Dataset.
- Baselines.
- Metrics.
- Experimental environment.

## Visual

Prefer:

- Table.
- Experimental pipeline.

---

# Slide 12: Main Results

## Content

Show:

- Main comparison results.
- Important observations.

## Visual

Priority:

1. Original paper table/figure.
2. Simplified visualization.

Explain:

- What improved?
- Why improved?
- What does it prove?

---

# Slide 13: Ablation Analysis

## Goal

Explain:

Which components matter?

## Visual

Use:

- Ablation table.
- Component comparison chart.

---

# Slide 14: Limitations and Discussion

## Content

Analyze:

- Method limitations.
- Dataset limitations.
- Generalization issues.

Avoid only repeating paper limitations.

Add:

- Personal research perspective.

---

# Slide 15: Conclusion

## Structure

Three sentences:

1. What problem was solved?
2. What method was proposed?
3. What was the main finding?

## Visual

Use:

- Summary diagram.
- Key takeaway list.

---

# Figure Usage Rules

For every paper presentation:

## Required

Use original paper figures whenever available.

Examples:

- Framework diagram.
- Dataset statistics.
- Experimental results.
- Architecture overview.

## Processing

Allowed:

- Crop.
- Highlight.
- Annotate.

Not allowed:

- Fabricate figures.
- Modify results.
- Generate fake experimental charts.

---

# Speaker Note Template

For each slide provide:

## Purpose

Why this slide exists.

## Explanation

How to explain the figure/content.

## Key Message

The one sentence audience should remember.

---

The generated PPT plan should include:


The final presentation should resemble a carefully prepared graduate research seminar presentation.
